You'd be thrown in a car, beaten, cut and dumped on a beach, and walk away. You'd remove the two most powerful bosses in New York in five months and rebuild the Mafia like a company. And the country you built it in? It puts you on a ship and sends you away for good.

This is the whole life of Charles "Lucky" Luciano, the Sicilian boy who built the modern American Mafia, from birth to the very end, told as if it were yours.
