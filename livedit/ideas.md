## What the comments say people love (use in every script)
- "Hearing it told as if it's you hits completely differently" — second person is the hook.
- Dark, painful backstories: a cruel father, betrayal by a friend/mentor, losing family, surviving the impossible
  (Ghost: buried alive; Kratos: lost brother, wife, kid). Comments retell these beats.
- Accuracy is checked hard ("you missed X", "OG vs reboot"): get facts right, mention what the sources disagree on.
- Little funny lines get quoted ("You try very hard not to start Ragnarok… You start Ragnarok", "and the kid? Gone.").
- Viewers come from Instagram Reels clips; a 54-second Short links to the full video.
- Ends with a question → comments explode ("Would you have talked?", "How do you think he got his mask?").
