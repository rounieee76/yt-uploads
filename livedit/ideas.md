## 2. What the comments say works (rules for every script)
1. **Second person, whole life from birth.** "Hearing it as if it's you hits differently."
2. **Quotable lines get the biggest likes.** Top examples: "I gave you all I had. I did." (168) and "and the kid? Gone." Fans also quote dry jokes, such as "Born as Ghost and still have to do homework" (304). Write 2–3 lines per script meant to be quoted.
3. **Callbacks and symmetry.** A fan timestamped the Tommy printing-press callback (dad at the presses → Tommy buys a print works). Plant something early and pay it off late.
4. **Clarity about the story is the main value** ("finally I understand the lore", 88 likes). Explain the world simply.
5. **Accuracy gets checked in every language:** dates, ages, who shot whom. Double-check years. Where sources disagree, say so.
6. **End on a question** ("How did Ghost get his mask?"). Debates get the most likes (OG vs reboot Ghost, 473).
7. **Dark backstory beats:** a cruel father, betrayal by the closest friend, loss of family, surviving the impossible.
8. **One consistent narrator.** People asked "what happened to the previous narrator?" Keep am_michael.
9. **Length:** two viewers asked for 15–20 minutes or 2–3 parts. Test one longer video or a 2-part story.
10. **Series around one world** ("one of the devils of 141… I'll wait for the others"). Plan linked episodes (e.g. Mongols → Kublai, the Wild West → Jesse James → Billy the Kid → Doc Holliday).
11. **Traffic comes from Reels/TikTok and Shorts.** Cut a 45–60 s Short of each video's strongest scene.
12. **Dubs are wanted:** Persian, Turkish, Russian, Spanish and Arabic comments are common. Add multi-language audio tracks later.
13. People openly say "vibecoded / which AI?". The art has to look hand-made and consistent; don't claim "all original" if it isn't.
