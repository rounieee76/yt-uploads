# Lived It format (studied from Born in Lore, Nov 2026) — our own words, our own art
- TITLE: "What If You Were Born as <Name>? | <World/Era>" (e.g. "| The Mongol Empire", "| Prohibition Chicago").
  Variant he tested on newest upload: "<Name>'s Whole Life, But It's Yours | <World>".
- LENGTH: 9–14 min, ~1,500–2,000 words, Kokoro voice. Prologue + 9 chapters "I · Short Title" … "IX · …".
- SCRIPT: second person, present tense, short punchy sentences.
  Prologue opens on birth with a triple "No X. No Y. Not even Z." beat, then a 4–6 sentence flash-forward of the
  most shocking beats, then "This is the whole life of <name>, from <start> to <end>, told as if it were yours."
  Each chapter = one turning point, ends on a turn or cliffhanger; occasional dry one-liners; the end lands on a
  quiet final image (a bench in the sun, a barn door) and a question to the viewer.
- VISUALS: flat 2D vector illustration, thick dark outlines, warm muted palettes, a small watermark top-left,
  simple motion (pans, pops, parallax, props sliding in), paper/card props (photos pinned to boards, stamped crates).
- THUMBNAIL (our version): split frame — left: cartoon baby, "AGE 0" label; right: the grown legend at his most iconic
  moment, a 1–3 word red hook ("BURIED ALIVE"-type). Our own drawings, different palette.
- DESCRIPTION: flash-forward paragraph in 2nd person; one line listing story beats; question + "Who should be born
  next? Tell me in the comments."; chapters; sources; "All illustrations, animation and music are original."
- TAGS: name, name + backstory/story/life, era terms, key people, "what if you were born as".
