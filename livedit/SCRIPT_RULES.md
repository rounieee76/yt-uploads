# Lived It script rules (from studying Born in Lore's top videos: Ghost 4.7M, CJ 2.1M, Kratos 1.4M)
Our own words always; only the structure is learned.

1. COLD OPEN (0:00-0:20): "If you were born as X, you'd <shocking fact 1>. Then <fact 2>. And <the person closest / the thing that ends you>? <twist>."
   Then: "But right now? You're a baby <place>. No A. No B. Not even C."
2. FAMILY in 30-60 s: names, one tiny real detail per person, a line of dialogue if a real one exists.
3. PLANTS: say "Remember that." after an early detail, pay it off later ("Remember the razor?").
   At least 3 plants per script, each paid off. The ending pays off the cold open.
4. SENTENCES: present tense, second person, 4-14 words. Fragments. Question then answer ("And the scared little boy? Gone.").
5. CHAPTERS: "Prologue: <place>" then I..IX with 1-3 word titles. A new chapter every 60-90 s.
6. MID-VIDEO PAUSE near 40-45%: who's next? ask for a name in comments, then subscribe. 15 s max, then a hard hook back in.
7. DARK BEATS: betrayal by the closest person, loss of family, surviving the impossible. Never skip the ugly part, never dwell on gore.
8. HUMOUR: 2-3 dry one-liners that are quotable.
9. ACCURACY: real dates, ages, names. If sources disagree, say so in one line ("Nobody agrees on why.").
10. ENDING: 3-sentence recap of losses and wins ("You lost X, Y and Z. And you never..."), pay off the opening, then ONE question that starts a debate.
11. LENGTH 9-13 min (1,500-2,100 words).
12. TITLE: "Live <Name>'s Entire Life in <N> Minutes" (his newest, 4.7M) or "What If You Were Born as <Name>? | <World>".
