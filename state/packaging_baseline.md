## What the data says
- The hit got 91% of its views from Browse (home feed): 327 views day 1, 2,143 day 2, 2,296 day 3, then the push stopped by day 5.
  Its CTR was 3.9% and relative retention only ~0.5 (below similar videos): 43% of viewers left in the first 10% (~55 s).
  => YouTube tested it widely, CTR was okay, retention was weak, so the test ended. Fix the first minute.
- The comeuppance videos never got the Browse test (398 and 834 impressions). Their retention per viewer is better (4:26-4:31).
  Dealerships CTR 2.2% = packaging problem. Car wash comeuppance CTR 4.3% = reach problem (topic repeat of the hit for the same viewers).
- Light-gray minimal thumbnails: 2.2% and 4.3% on tiny samples. Dark/saturated photo + question thumbnails: 3.9% and 4.3% on large samples.
  Until a light-gray one beats 4% on 1,000+ impressions, default to the hit's style: photo scene, high contrast, a 2-4 word question or claim.
