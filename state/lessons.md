# Story lessons from our own analytics (read before writing every script)

1. **The first 60 seconds lose half the audience.** Car wash hit (DjbxaXK8hcw): 43% gone by 10% of the video (~55 s),
   relative retention ~0.5 (worse than half of similar videos). That is why YouTube stopped pushing it after 3 days
   despite 34K impressions. Rules: the first sentence restates the title/thumbnail promise in plain words; the first
   15 s show the price or number from the thumbnail on screen; open a question in the first 20 s that is only answered
   at the end; no slow "in the news" montage before the promise; no channel intro.
2. **Comeuppance videos hold viewers longer once they click** (avg 4:26–4:31 vs 3:03) — the story structure works; the
   problem there was reach and packaging, not the script.
