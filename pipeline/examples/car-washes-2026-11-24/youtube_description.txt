DESCRIPTION

On February 5th, 2025, Zips Car Wash walked into a bankruptcy court in Dallas owing $654 million. Twenty days later, Driven Brands announced it was selling its entire U.S. car wash chain. Then the auto-renewal lawsuits arrived. And in May 2026, Mister Car Wash, the biggest chain in America, left the stock market at $7 a share, less than half of its $15 IPO price.

Yet Americans are paying for more car wash memberships than ever.

So what really happened? This video follows the money: how a monthly plan turned car washes into "software with soap," why private equity built roughly 900 new washes a year, what one wash actually costs the operator, and who is paying the bill now. Debt, customers or Wall Street?
