On May 31, 2023, Advance Auto Parts cut its quarterly dividend from $1.50 a share to 25 cents, and its stock fell about 35% in a single day. That September, S&P cut its debt to junk and the CEO left. In February 2024, it told the SEC its annual report would be late after finding errors in its own books. In November 2024, it announced it would close 523 stores. Meanwhile, Americans are keeping their cars longer than ever, and AutoZone and O'Reilly keep growing.

So what really happened? This is the story of how the company that set out to be the biggest parts seller in North America fell behind, how a parts store actually makes money, and why the bill finally came due.
